Reading the rail

  • Layers stack bottom-up in the order they went onto the canvas. Solid black foot with a hatched band over it is the combination that keeps turning up: dry black ground, Clear on top, transparent colour, then the whites.

What the cues teach

  • Fourteen of twenty are specialty preparations. The fragments that found them were almost never technique words — they were shape, colour and position. "Three trees breaking the vignette on the right" outperforms any archetype tag in the index.
