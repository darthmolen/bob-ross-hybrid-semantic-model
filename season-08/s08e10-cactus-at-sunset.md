# Season 8, Episode 10 — "Cactus at Sunset" (1987)

## 1. Composition
- Desert landscape dominated by dramatic sunset sky
- Rolling desert floor with textured sand and rocky outcroppings
- Multiple tall saguaro cacti standing as dark silhouettes on the right
- Brilliant sun breaking through heavy cloud cover at center-left horizon
- No water features or structures
A desert sunset composition with cacti silhouettes against an intensely dramatic sky.

## 2. Palette
- Deep crimsons and scarlets throughout the cloud layers
- Bright oranges and coral pinks in mid-sky transitions
- Brilliant yellow-white sun as focal point
- Browns, tans, and siennas in the desert floor
- Dark umbers and near-blacks for cactus silhouettes and shadows
Palette identity: **Fiery desert twilight**

## 3. Mood & Atmosphere
- Dramatic and intense emotional quality
- Otherworldly, almost apocalyptic desert atmosphere
- Strong contrast between bright sky and darkening land
- Sense of heat and vastness
Lighting: **Backlighting** from low sunset creating stark silhouettes against brilliant sky

## 4. Structural Layout
- **Left:** Dramatic red-orange clouds with sun emerging, rocky desert formations
- **Right:** Cluster of tall saguaro cacti standing as dark silhouettes
- **Center:** Brilliant sun breaking through cloud cover at the horizon line
- **Foreground:** Textured desert floor with shadows and dimensional ridges
- **Midground:** Rock formations and desert terrain rising toward horizon
- **Background:** Heavily clouded sky with intense red-orange gradient

Depth style: **Atmospheric perspective with silhouette**

## 5. Motion
- Strong horizontal cloud bands sweeping across the sky
- Radiating light from the central sun
- Implied wind movement in cloud formations
- Stillness in the cacti and land contrasting with active sky
Motion profile: **Horizontal sweep with radiant burst**

## 6. Technique
- Wet-on-wet blending for dramatic cloud layers
- Fan brush for cactus silhouettes
- Knife work for textured desert floor and rock formations
- Pulling technique for horizontal cloud bands
- Light glazing for luminous sun glow effect
Signature technique: **Wet-on-wet atmospheric blending**

## 7. Narrative Layer
"A southwestern desert holds its breath at the day's most dramatic moment. The setting sun transforms ordinary clouds into curtains of fire, casting ancient cacti into stark relief. The landscape seems caught between worlds—the cool darkness creeping across the sand and the blazing farewell of light above."

## 8. Initial Canvas Treatment

- **Black gesso** brushed over the canvas and allowed to **dry completely** before any oil is applied. This is a dry acrylic ground, not a wet one: the oils above it cannot be pulled back into it, and every light value in the painting has to be added rather than lifted.
- No Liquid Clear and no Liquid White base — the oils are carried thin directly over the ground.
- **Contact paper** cut to shape and applied as a mask, so the area it covers stays clean while the surround is painted, and is lifted before that area is worked.

The black ground carries the value structure of the whole painting. Read the light in this entry as light recovered from darkness, not as shadow laid over white.
